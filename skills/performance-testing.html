02 Experience

Twenty years of making systems fast enough

I started as a performance tester on the Norwich Union insurance platform in 2001 and went on to consult, teach and manage performance testing across banking, payments, telecoms and the public sector.

At Monitise I grew the performance testing team from one person to nine while staying 90% hands-on, and set the company-wide performance standards. Most recently, at Ten10, I used JMeter to find infrastructure bottlenecks and improve the stability of enterprise systems.

My years as an Environment Manager mean I understand the infrastructure under the application, which makes it quicker to work out where a bottleneck really is.
